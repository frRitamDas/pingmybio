FAQ

Questions before you start

Is PingMyBio free to start?
Yes. The Free plan includes 300 AI replies a month, up to 5 live offers, unlimited links and click analytics — no card needed. You only upgrade when your reply volume outgrows it.
What does the AI assistant answer?
Whatever you teach it: your FAQs, how you work, your offers and availability. It answers in your voice and points visitors to the right offer, booking or contact step instead of guessing.
What counts as an AI reply?
One reply your assistant gives a fan on your page is one AI reply. Offer cards shown inside a reply aren't charged extra, and brand research runs when a partner starts a collab — separate from your reply meter.
Can I add offers and bookings?
Yes. Add offers as cards on your page and your assistant will recommend them in conversation. Where bookings are enabled, visitors can pick a session and confirm without leaving your link.
Can brands contact me?
On Starter and Pro, "Work with me" gives brands a dedicated request path. You get their brief plus research and a suggested reply in your Inbox, so you can respond with context.
What happens when I hit my reply limit?
Your page stays up and your offers keep working — you can upgrade to a bigger reply allowance at any time, or wait for your allowance to reset. You can switch or cancel plans from Settings → Billing.
