FAQ

Common questions

Is PingMyBio free to start?
Yes. The Free plan is $0 and includes 300 AI replies a month and up to 5 live offers. You can upgrade anytime from Settings under Billing.
What does the AI assistant answer?
Questions about you, using the information you add: your FAQs, notes, services, and offers. It can point visitors to the right offer or next step, such as booking a session or buying a product.
What counts as an AI reply?
One reply your assistant gives a visitor on your page counts as one AI reply. Offer cards included in a reply don't cost extra. Brand research for collaborations doesn't count toward your AI replies.
Can I add offers and bookings?
Yes. Add your offers to your page and your assistant can recommend them. The Free plan includes up to 5 live offers, and Starter and Pro include unlimited offers. Booking and buying are available where enabled.
Can brands contact me?
Yes, on Starter and Pro. Brands can send collaboration requests from your page. When one arrives, you get AI brand research and a draft reply brief in your Inbox.
