Best Linktree alternatives in 2026

People searching “Linktree alternative” rarely want a prettier button page. They want a bio that answers, books, and sells. Here’s the honest shortlist — and how to pick. 2026·09·09 · 11 min read

Google’s first result for Linktree alternative is still a Reddit thread: “Best link in bio tool / Linktree alternative?” That is the real brief. Creators aren’t shopping for a better-looking list. They’re trying to stop losing DMs, bookings, and brand asks after someone taps the bio. PingMyBio is our product — an intelligent bio hub, not a Linktree clone — but this roundup covers the whole field fairly. Verify current pricing on each vendor before you pay; fees move.

Short answer: Keep Linktree if you only need a list. Switch if your bio has to answer, book, or sell without sending people through three other apps.

Why people look for a Linktree alternative

  • Followers still DM “how much / what’s included / can we collab?” the moment the list can’t answer.
  • Selling and booking hop out to Gumroad, Calendly, Stan, or a form — the bio is a parking lot, not a storefront.
  • Lower paid tiers have historically taken a cut of digital sales (Linktree published 12% on Free and 9% on mid plans, with 0% only on the top tier — confirm live).
  • A list can’t recommend the right offer at 1am. A person-shaped assistant can.

How to pick: ignore the 20-tool listicles

Match the tool to the job, then stop reading:

  • Pretty links, lowest effort — Linktree, Lnk.Bio, Carrd
  • Personal brand one-pager — Solo
  • Media kit and brand deals — Beacons
  • Fast digital checkout — Stan Store
  • A bio that answers, routes offers, and takes collabs — PingMyBio

The alternatives at a glance

PingMyBio — the intelligent bio

An assistant grounded in your knowledge answers fans 24/7, recommends your offers, and takes Work with me requests from brands (with research and a reply brief in your Inbox). Free tier with 300 AI replies/month; paid plans from $12. 0% sales fees. Pick it if your bio is how you get clients.

Solo — the personal site

A clean personal-brand page with links and a little identity design. Great taste, minimal commerce. Pick it if you want a beautiful “about me” URL and sells nothing directly.

Beacons — the creator suite

Store, email, media kit, links — broad toolkit, and the free plan has its own fee posture. Pick it for the media kit if brands ask for one constantly.

Stan Store — the mobile checkout

One-tap digital products and sessions for TikTok/IG sellers. Paid from day one; real value when your funnel is a single product.

Lnk.Bio — the cheap list

Very low cost, sometimes lifetime deals. Fine for a pure list. Thin for a business that needs conversation and routing.

Carrd — the blank canvas

A one-page site builder pretending to be a link in bio. Maximum flexibility, maximum fiddling. Pick it if you enjoy CSS more than sales.

The honest decision tree

  1. Do you sell anything, or get asked “how much?” weekly? If no → Linktree/Lnk.Bio, done.
  2. Mostly brand/media-kit questions? → Beacons or PingMyBio’s Work with me.
  3. One digital product, one price, one page? → Stan Store or Payhip.
  4. Coaching, services, workshops — anything with a conversation before the sale? → PingMyBio. The assistant pre-qualifies while you sleep.
Migration tip: whatever you choose, keep the slug people already know, move your top three links first, and add measurement (click analytics or UTM) on day one. You can’t improve what you never measure.
