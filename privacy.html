Privacy
Policy.

How PingMyBio collects, uses, and protects information — for sellers, buyers, and visitors. Last updated — October 28, 2026

PingMyBio (“PingMyBio,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates pingmybio.com and related applications that provide an AI link-in-bio / creator storefront — product listings, bookings, courses, checkout, and seller tools (the “Service”). It is for transparency, including for third-party sign-in providers such as Google, and is not legal advice.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Sellers — creators and businesses who create accounts, configure storefronts, and list products or services.
  • Buyers — people who browse public storefronts, purchase, book, or chat with a store’s assistant.
  • Visitors — people who view our marketing site or public storefronts without signing in.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and profile

Email address, authentication data (including Google Sign-In), store name, handle/username, bio, avatar, social links, and branding you choose to provide. Authentication is provided through our infrastructure partner, Supabase; Google shares basic profile information per your Google account permissions and Google’s privacy policy.

2.2 Store, product, and commerce data

Sellers may upload product titles, descriptions, prices, images, FAQs, and booking settings. Buyers may provide name, email, and order notes needed to complete a purchase, delivery, or booking.

2.3 AI chat data

On AI storefronts, buyers may message an assistant grounded in seller-provided listings and FAQs. We store chat messages and session identifiers to deliver the conversation, maintain reliability and security, and — where available — help sellers understand buyer questions. Don’t submit sensitive personal data you don’t want processed for these purposes.

2.4 Usage and technical data

Pages and storefronts viewed, product interactions (opening a product, starting checkout), device and browser type, and a one-way hash of IP mixed with browser details — not the raw IP.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and secure the Service — auth, pages, checkout, bookings, chat.
  • Operate seller tools — analytics, inbox, collab briefs, assistant training.
  • Communicate — receipts, Inbox alerts, security notices, and (with consent) product updates.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not place third-party advertising trackers on public storefronts.

4. How we share

  • Infrastructure — hosting, auth, email, and payment processors under contract (for example, Supabase and Stripe-class providers).
  • Sellers — buyers interact with a seller’s storefront; sellers receive the details needed to fulfill orders and answer requests.
  • Legal — when required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the Service.
  • In connection with a business transfer — merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice where required.

5. Retention and security

We keep information as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege practices. No system is perfectly secure; report concerns to privacy@pingmybio.com.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access, correct, export, or delete your account data from settings, or by writing to us.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing where the law (GDPR/UK DPA/CCPA-style rights) applies.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing email via the footer link in any message.
  • Buyers: contact the seller for order data; contact us for platform data.

7. Cookies

We use a minimal set of strictly necessary cookies for session and security. No cross-site ad cookies. If we add optional analytics cookies, we’ll ask first.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age of digital consent where you live). We don’t knowingly collect their data; contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. International transfers

We process data in the regions where our infrastructure providers operate. Where data leaves your region, we rely on appropriate safeguards (for example, standard contractual clauses).

10. Changes

We’ll post updates here and, for material changes, notify sellers by email or in-app notice before they take effect.

11. Contact

PingMyBio — privacy@pingmybio.com. For security reports: security@pingmybio.com.
