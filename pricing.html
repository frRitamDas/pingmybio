FAQ

Billing questions

What counts as one AI reply?
One reply your assistant gives a fan on your page. Offer cards shown inside a reply are not charged extra.
Is brand research part of the reply meter?
No. Brand research runs when a partner starts a collab request, and it is counted separately from your monthly reply allowance.
What happens when I hit the limit?
Your page and offers stay online. You can upgrade for a bigger allowance right away, or wait for your allowance to reset at the start of your billing period. Nothing is deleted.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from Settings → Billing at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply from your next billing period.
Do you put ads or Google Analytics on my page?
No. Your page shows your content and your offers. Click analytics are first-party and shown inside your workspace.
How are yearly plans billed?
Yearly billing charges once upfront — $120 for Starter and $290 for Pro — which works out to $10 and $24.17 per month. That is the 17% saving shown in the toggle.
